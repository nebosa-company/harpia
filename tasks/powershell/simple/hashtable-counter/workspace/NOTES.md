# Token frequency report

The report is pasted into the weekly summary by hand, so two runs over
the same corpus must produce byte-identical text. Hash tables do not
promise an enumeration order, which is exactly why the ordering rules for
the report are spelled out rather than left to whatever comes out of the
table.

Shares are always relative to the whole corpus, not to the slice that
made the cut — a top-3 table whose shares add up to 1.00 would be a lie.

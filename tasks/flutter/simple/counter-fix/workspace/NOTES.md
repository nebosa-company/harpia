# Tally

Small demo counter used in onboarding material. The display bug appeared
after a hasty cleanup pass; the buttons are wired to the right handlers.

There are no platform folders here on purpose — the app is exercised through
widget-level runs only, so `lib/` and the pubspec are the whole story.

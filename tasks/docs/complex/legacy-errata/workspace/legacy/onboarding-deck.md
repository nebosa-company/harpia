# Onboarding deck, speaker notes

Speaker notes from the platform onboarding session. Written once and
presented about thirty times since, without being revisited.

## Slide 4: what we keep

Operational logs are retained for 180 days. Access logs are retained for
400 days. Anything with a customer identifier on it comes out on the
shorter clock.

## Slide 7: exports

An export whose archive would exceed 5 GB is split into parts. Warn
customers about this before they build anything on top of the export,
because the surprise is what generates the ticket, not the splitting.

## Slide 9: the rota

Two people are on the rota at any time, primary and secondary. Handover
is on Monday morning.

New joiners shadow the rota in their second week without carrying the
pager.

## Slide 12: what to read next

The service policy is the contractual document. The export runbook is
the operational one. This deck is neither and should not be quoted at a
customer.

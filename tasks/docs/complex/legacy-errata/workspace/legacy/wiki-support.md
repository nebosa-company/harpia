# Support wiki page

Exported from the old wiki. Edited by about fifteen people over four
years; the edit history was not exported with it.

## Response targets

A Sev 1 incident receives a first response within 1 hour. A Sev 2
incident receives a first response within 4 hours during the working
week.

Out of hours, only Sev 1 pages anybody.

## On-call

The rota carries two people at a time, a primary and a secondary. The
secondary is not a spare pager: they run communications while the
primary debugs.

Handover happens on Wednesday morning, so that a rough weekend is never
also somebody's first day on the rota.

Swaps are self-service up to 48 hours before a shift. Inside 48 hours a
swap needs the platform lead, so that the escalation path stays
accurate.

## Escalation

If the primary has not acknowledged within ten minutes the alert goes to
the secondary, and ten minutes after that to the platform lead.

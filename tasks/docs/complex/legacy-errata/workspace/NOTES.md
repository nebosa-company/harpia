# Documentation clean-up notes

Six documents, one subject, six years of nobody owning it. The merge is
the easy half. The half that matters is the errata: the people who have
been reading the wrong document for a year need to know which fact they
have wrong, not just that a new document exists.

An errata entry that is not a real contradiction is worse than a missing
one. It sends somebody to check a document that was right all along, and
the next time they will not check.

`precedence.md` is the agreed tie-breaker. It was agreed precisely so
that this exercise does not turn into an argument about whose document
is better.

# Audit preparation notes

The auditor works from the checklist, not from the policy documents, so
the checklist has to be complete in both directions. A policy with no
row is a control we cannot show we operate. A row for a policy that was
withdrawn is worse: we would be asked for evidence of something nobody
does any more, and the answer would be that the whole document set is
unreliable.

Withdrawn policies stay in their documents for the audit trail. They are
not part of the control set and they do not satisfy anything.

The control catalogue is the other half of the picture. A control that
no live policy claims is a gap, and the auditor will find it whether or
not we list it, so we list it.

# Support ticket analysis, 2025 Q4

Every ticket raised in the quarter was tagged by the support team using
the standard taxonomy. This note covers the export-related subset.

## Volume

The quarter produced 4,318 tickets in total. Of those, 137 mentioned an
export timing out, and a further 96 asked how long an export was
expected to take. Together that is 5.4 percent of all tickets and the
largest single cause after billing questions.

The 137 timeout tickets are concentrated: 41 accounts raised them, and
six accounts raised more than five each.

## What the timeout tickets actually were

Reading the 137 tickets, 84 of them describe a run that had not failed
at all. The customer had waited past the point where they expected an
answer, assumed the run was stuck, and opened a ticket. In 62 of those
84 cases the export completed successfully while the ticket was open.

That is the shape of the problem. The platform is not failing these
customers; it is failing to tell them anything.

## Handling cost

Median first-response time on these tickets is 3.2 hours and median time
to close is 19 hours, almost all of which is waiting to see whether the
run finishes.

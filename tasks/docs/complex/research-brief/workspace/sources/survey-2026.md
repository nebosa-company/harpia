# Customer survey, January 2026

Sent to 1,840 workspace administrators, 611 usable responses, response
rate 33 percent. Questions about the export workflow were asked of every
respondent; the free-text box was optional and 214 people used it.

## Which step of your weekly routine takes the longest?

| Step | Share of respondents |
| --- | --- |
| Running the data export | 62% |
| Reconciling against the ledger | 19% |
| Preparing the upload | 11% |
| Something else | 8% |

Sixty-two percent of respondents named the data export as the slowest
part of their week, which is more than the other three answers put
together.

## How predictable is the export?

| Answer | Share of respondents |
| --- | --- |
| I can predict it within a few minutes | 14% |
| I can predict it within an hour | 31% |
| I cannot predict it at all | 55% |

## Free text, most frequent themes

The 214 free-text answers were coded into themes by two researchers
independently, agreeing on 91 percent of assignments.

The largest theme, at 88 mentions, was not knowing whether a run had
stalled or was simply still working. The second, at 51 mentions, was the
absence of any estimate at the start of a run.

Only 12 mentions asked for the export to be faster.

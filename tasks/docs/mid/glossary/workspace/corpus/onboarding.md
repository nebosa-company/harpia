# Onboarding

Week one is deliberately slow. You pair for four days before you touch a
repository alone, and your first change is a documentation fix so that
the release path is familiar before it matters.

In week two you shadow whoever is on the on-call rota. You do not carry
the pager and you are not expected to fix anything; you watch how an
alert turns into a decision, and you read the runbook that the person
opens when it does.

Before the end of your first month you will have watched at least one
canary deploy promote and at least one roll back. Ask about the
golden signal thresholds while you are watching, because the numbers
are the part nobody writes down.

The incident commander role is not open to you until you have been
through an incident as a participant, which usually takes a quarter.

# Glossary notes

The glossary exists because the same five words mean different things in
different teams here. Its value is that every entry is anchored: a reader
who does not believe a definition can open the documents it points at and
argue with the source rather than with the glossary.

`terms.txt` is the list somebody asked for. Not every term on it is
actually used in the handbook, and a term nobody uses is worth knowing
about too — a definition invented for it would be the only place it
exists.

# Quarterly Business Review, 2025 Q2

Prepared by finance and platform. Figures here are final; a later review
restates a figure only by saying so explicitly.

## Headline metrics

| Metric | Value |
| --- | --- |
| ARR (EUR m) | 19.2 |
| Active enterprise accounts | 384 |
| Net revenue retention | 110% |
| Platform availability | 99.95% |
| Engineering headcount | 84 |
| Support tickets raised | 1318 |

## Commentary

ARR crossed 19 million in the second month of the quarter and finished at
19.2. Growth is still overwhelmingly expansion rather than new logos: 23
net new enterprise accounts took the base to 384.

Platform availability of 99.95% is the best quarter the platform has ever
had, and the first time the target has been met exactly rather than
missed. There was no Sev 1 incident in the quarter, which has not
happened before.

Engineering headcount reached 84. The six joiners were all backfills or
plan hires; no requisition was opened outside the plan.

Support ticket volume rose to 1318, tracking the account base rather than
any product regression. The mix moved towards integration questions,
which the team reads as a sign that customers are deploying more widely.

## Risks

Retention at 110% flatters a quarter in which nothing churned. Two
accounts at renewal in Q3 are each large enough to move the number by a
point on their own.

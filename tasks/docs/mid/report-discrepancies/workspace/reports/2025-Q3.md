# Quarterly Business Review, 2025 Q3

Prepared by finance and platform. Figures here are final; a later review
restates a figure only by saying so explicitly.

## Headline metrics

| Metric | Value |
| --- | --- |
| ARR (EUR m) | 20.1 |
| Active enterprise accounts | 412 |
| Net revenue retention | 111% |
| Platform availability | 99.88% |
| Engineering headcount | 89 |
| Support tickets raised | 1290 |

## Commentary

Both renewals flagged in the previous review closed, and both expanded.
ARR reached 20.1 million and the enterprise base finished the quarter at
412 active accounts, 28 more than at the end of Q2.

Platform availability of 99.88% is the weakest quarter of the year so
far. Two incidents account for it: a session-store eviction on 14 August
and a slow read-replica failover on 2 September.

Engineering headcount is 89. Five joiners, no leavers, and the two SRE
requisitions that opened in Q2 are both still unfilled.

Support ticket volume fell slightly to 1290 despite the larger base,
which the support lead attributes to the self-service export feature
shipped in July.

## Risks

The two open SRE requisitions are now the constraint on the on-call rota
rather than a nice-to-have. If neither is filled by the end of Q4 the
secondary rotation drops below the agreed minimum.

# Quarterly Business Review, 2025 Q4

Prepared by finance and platform. Figures here are final; a later review
restates a figure only by saying so explicitly.

## Headline metrics

| Metric | Value |
| --- | --- |
| ARR (EUR m) | 21.6 |
| Active enterprise accounts | 437 |
| Net revenue retention | 113% |
| Platform availability | 99.97% |
| Engineering headcount | 96 |
| Support tickets raised | 1402 |

## Commentary

A strong close. ARR finished at 21.6 million. Enterprise accounts grew
from 418 at the end of Q3 to 437 at the end of Q4, and net revenue
retention of 113% is the highest the company has recorded.

Platform availability of 99.97% is a new best. The capacity work that
followed the August session-store incident is the single largest reason;
the December peak passed without an incident of any severity.

Engineering headcount closed the year at 96. Both SRE requisitions were
filled in November, which takes the secondary rotation back above the
agreed minimum.

Support ticket volume of 1402 is the highest of the year, in line with
the December peak and the larger account base.

## Year in review

| Quarter | ARR (EUR m) | Platform availability | Engineering headcount |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2025 Q1 | 18.4 | 99.91% | 78 |
| 2025 Q2 | 19.2 | 99.87% | 84 |
| 2025 Q3 | 20.1 | 99.88% | 89 |
| 2025 Q4 | 21.6 | 99.97% | 96 |

ARR grew by 17% over the year. Availability was below target in three
quarters of four and the fourth quarter is the only one anybody wants to
repeat.

## Risks

Net revenue retention of 113% is a peak, not a plateau. The renewal book
for Q1 2026 is thinner and the number is expected to give back a point.

# Quarterly Business Review, 2026 Q1

Prepared by finance and platform. Figures here are final; a later review
restates a figure only by saying so explicitly.

## Headline metrics

| Metric | Value |
| --- | --- |
| ARR (EUR m) | 22.9 |
| Active enterprise accounts | 452 |
| Net revenue retention | 112% |
| Platform availability | 99.93% |
| Engineering headcount | 99 |
| Support tickets raised | 1357 |

## Commentary

ARR reached 22.9 million. The enterprise base moved from 437 at the end
of last year to 452, a smaller step than the previous two quarters and
in line with a thinner renewal book.

Net revenue retention came in at 112%, one point below the Q4 peak and
exactly where the previous review said it would land.

Platform availability of 99.93% missed the target by two hundredths.
A single incident on 6 February, a bad configuration push to the edge
tier, accounts for the whole shortfall.

Engineering headcount at the start of the year was 91 and finished the
quarter at 99. All eight joiners were plan hires against the 2026 budget
rather than carry-over requisitions from last year.

Support ticket volume of 1357 is down on the December peak and up on the
same quarter last year, which is what a larger base with a stable product
looks like.

## Risks

The renewal book for Q2 2026 contains the two largest accounts on the
book at the same time. Nothing suggests either is at risk, but the
concentration is worth naming before the quarter rather than after it.

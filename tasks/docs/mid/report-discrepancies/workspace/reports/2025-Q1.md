# Quarterly Business Review, 2025 Q1

Prepared by finance and platform. Figures here are final; a later review
restates a figure only by saying so explicitly.

## Headline metrics

| Metric | Value |
| --- | --- |
| ARR (EUR m) | 18.4 |
| Active enterprise accounts | 361 |
| Net revenue retention | 108% |
| Platform availability | 99.91% |
| Engineering headcount | 78 |
| Support tickets raised | 1204 |

## Commentary

The quarter opened with the tail of the December enterprise push still
converting, which is most of the 361 active enterprise accounts. Two of
the three largest new accounts came through the partner channel, which
had contributed nothing at all a year earlier.

Net revenue retention of 108% is the number the board watches. It is
carried by seat expansion in existing accounts rather than by price, and
that distinction matters: seat expansion tracks our customers' hiring,
which is not something we control.

Platform availability of 99.91% sits below the 99.95% target. The whole
gap is one incident on 18 February, when a certificate rotation took the
public API offline for 41 minutes.

Engineering headcount closed at 78 against a plan of 82. Two offers were
declined and one requisition was withdrawn when the roadmap moved.

## Risks

The partner channel is now large enough that losing one partner would be
visible in the ARR line. Nobody is arguing to slow it down, but the
concentration should be measured from next quarter.

Support ticket volume of 1204 is up only slightly on Q4 and is not yet a
staffing concern.

# Purchase order number not shown on the invoice PDF

## Customer

"Our finance system rejects any invoice without our PO number on the
face of the document. We enter it in the billing profile but it only
appears in the emailed summary, not in the PDF."

## Resolution

Reproduced. The PO field is stored on the billing profile and rendered
into the notification email but not into the PDF template. This needs a
template change and a re-render of the current period's documents.

Waiting on the billing team. No workaround offered yet, so nothing here
is settled.

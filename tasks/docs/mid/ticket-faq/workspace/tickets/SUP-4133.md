# Which addresses do webhook calls originate from

## Customer

"Our security team will not open the firewall to a hostname. They need
address ranges before they will allow inbound webhook traffic."

## Resolution

Webhook deliveries originate from two published ranges, 203.0.113.0/24
and 198.51.100.0/24. They have not changed since 2023 and any change is
announced at least 30 days in advance on the status page.

Sent both ranges and the status page subscription link. The customer's
security team allowed them the same day.

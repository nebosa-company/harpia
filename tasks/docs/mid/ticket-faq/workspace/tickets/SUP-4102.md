# Unexpected extra charge after adding users

## Customer

"I added four people to the workspace last week and today there is a
charge I did not expect. Is this a mistake?"

## Resolution

Same situation as SUP-4101: adding seats mid-period produces a prorated
charge covering the remainder of the period, billed separately from the
subscription line.

Confirmed the four seats were added on the 11th and the prorated amount
matches the days remaining. No refund due.

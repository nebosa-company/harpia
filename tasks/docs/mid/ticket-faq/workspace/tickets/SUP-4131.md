# Duplicate records created from our webhook consumer

## Customer

"Roughly one in fifty events shows up twice in our warehouse. Is the
platform sending them twice or are we doing something wrong?"

## Resolution

Both, in a sense. The platform does re-send, because delivery is
at-least-once, and the consumer is expected to de-duplicate on the
`delivery_id` header rather than assume exactly-once arrival.

Same underlying answer as SUP-4130.

# How long does the platform keep retrying a failed webhook

## Customer

"Our endpoint was down for about ten minutes during a deploy. We want to
know whether we lost those events or whether they came back."

## Resolution

A failed delivery is retried five times after the first attempt, spaced
1, 2, 4, 8 and 15 minutes apart, so the last retry lands 30 minutes
after the original attempt. Anything still failing then is marked
undelivered and shown in the integration log.

The customer's ten-minute outage falls comfortably inside that window,
so every event was redelivered. Nothing was lost.

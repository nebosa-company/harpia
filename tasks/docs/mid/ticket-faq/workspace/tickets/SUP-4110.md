# Transferring account ownership to a colleague

## Customer

"The person who set up our account has left. How do we move ownership to
someone still here?"

## Resolution

The current owner starts the transfer themselves; support does not do it
on their behalf. The person receiving ownership must already hold the
admin role on the account, so if they do not, they are promoted to admin
first and the transfer is offered afterwards.

The change takes effect immediately and the previous owner is left with
the admin role rather than being removed.

Where the original owner is unreachable, the transfer is handled through
the account's registered billing contact instead, which is a slower
manual process.

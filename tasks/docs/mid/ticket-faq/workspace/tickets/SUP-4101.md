# Invoice amount changed in the middle of the month

## Customer

"We were billed 1,180 euro on the 3rd and then a second, smaller amount
on the 19th. Nothing changed on our side that we know of and the second
invoice has a line we do not recognise."

## Resolution

Seats were added on the 18th. Seat changes are prorated to the day, so
the customer is charged for the part of the period the extra seats were
actually held. The prorated amount appears on the next invoice as its
own line rather than being folded into the subscription line, which is
why it looked unfamiliar.

Nothing to fix. Explained the proration rule and pointed at the seat
history in the account.

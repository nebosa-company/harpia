# Export produced several files instead of one

## Customer

"We asked for a full history export and got back six files rather than
the single archive we expected. Are they all different, or did something
go wrong?"

## Resolution

Nothing went wrong. An export whose total size would exceed 2 GB is
written out in parts of 500 MB each, numbered in order. The parts are
sequential slices of the same archive, not alternative copies, and they
are concatenated back together before extracting.

The customer's export was 2.7 GB, which is six parts.

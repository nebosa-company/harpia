# Request to re-issue an invoice with a corrected address

## Customer

"Our registered office moved in January and three invoices went out with
the old address. Our auditors want them re-issued."

## Resolution

An invoice can be re-issued with corrected details while it is still
inside the same tax year as the original. All three fall inside the
current year, so they were re-issued with the new registered address and
the same invoice numbers.

Once an invoice is outside its tax year it cannot be re-issued at all.
The correction is made with a credit note against the original and a
fresh invoice, which is what the customer's auditors will expect to see.

# Why is my download split into part-01, part-02 and so on

## Customer

"The download page lists four files with -part-01 through -part-04 in
the names. Which one do I actually need?"

## Resolution

All of them. Same as SUP-4120: large exports are split into 500 MB
parts once the archive would go over 2 GB, and every part is needed
because they are slices of one archive.

Sent the customer the concatenation command for their platform.

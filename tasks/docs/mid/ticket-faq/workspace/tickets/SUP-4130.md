# The same webhook event arrives twice

## Customer

"We are seeing the same order-created event delivered two or three
times. Our system creates a duplicate record every time."

## Resolution

Delivery is at-least-once by design, so a duplicate delivery is normal
and not a bug. Every delivery carries a `delivery_id` header that is
stable across retries of the same event; consumers are expected to
record it and ignore a repeat.

Walked the customer through keying their idempotency table on
`delivery_id` rather than on the event payload, which they were hashing.

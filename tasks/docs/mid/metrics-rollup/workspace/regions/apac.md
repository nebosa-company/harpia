# APAC pipeline, 2026 Q1

Compiled by the APAC marketing operations team on 2 April 2026. Figures
cover the quarter to 31 March.

Note on units: this region has always reported bookings in **thousands**
of euro, because the finance system APAC inherited rounds to the nearest
thousand. Every other region reports whole euro.

## Pipeline by market

| Market | Visits | Signups | Paid conversions | Bookings (EUR thousands) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Japan | 143900 | 4120 | 505 | 421.5 |
| Australia | 88250 | 2640 | 331 | 268.0 |
| Singapore | 51200 | 1780 | 244 | 196.5 |

## Commentary

Japan carries the region on volume; Singapore carries it on efficiency,
converting a visit to a paid customer at nearly twice the Japanese rate
off a much smaller base.

Australia was flat on the previous quarter and is the only market in the
region where signups fell.

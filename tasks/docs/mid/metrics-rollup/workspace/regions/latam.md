cover the quarter to 31 March. Bookings are in whole euro.

| Market | Visits | Signups | Paid conversions | Bookings (EUR) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Brazil | 78400 | 2050 | 214 | 158900 |

## Commentary

Chile opened in February and has not converted a paid customer yet. The
260 signups are real and the pipeline review expects the first paid
conversions in Q2, so the market stays in the table at zero rather than
being left out.

Brazil and Mexico both grew, Brazil faster, and neither has yet seen the
partner channel contribute anything measurable.

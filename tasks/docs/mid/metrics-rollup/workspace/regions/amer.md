# AMER pipeline, 2026 Q1

Compiled by the AMER marketing operations team on 4 April 2026. Figures
cover the quarter to 31 March. Bookings are in euro at the rate fixed for
the quarter.

## Pipeline by market

| Market | Visits | Signups | Paid conversions | Bookings (EUR) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| United States | 412600 | 14820 | 2106 | 1874500 |
| Canada | 96400 | 3210 | 428 | 372800 |

## Commentary

The United States remains the single largest market on every measure and
the gap widened this quarter. Canada's paid conversion rate is the higher
of the two, which is a mix effect: almost all Canadian traffic arrives
through the partner directory rather than paid search.

Nothing in this report is adjusted for seasonality.

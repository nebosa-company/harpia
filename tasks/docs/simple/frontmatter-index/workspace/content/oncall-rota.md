# On-call Rota

Two people are on the rota at any time: primary and secondary. The
secondary is not a spare pager, they are the person who runs comms while
the primary debugs.

Swaps are self-service up to 48 hours before a shift. Inside 48 hours the
swap needs the platform lead so the escalation path stays accurate.

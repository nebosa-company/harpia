# Renewal notice notes

The renewal notice is a contractual communication. Legal signed off on
the template wording and it does not get improved, shortened or made
friendlier — the only thing that changes between accounts is the values
that fill the placeholders.

An account with a hole in its record does not get a letter with a guess
in it. It gets held back and chased.

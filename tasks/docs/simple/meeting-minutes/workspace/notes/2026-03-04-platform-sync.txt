--- 1. Postgres 16 upgrade ---

dm walked through the staging soak: eleven days on the new cluster, no
regressions in the nightly query suite, p99 on the checkout path down from
84 ms to 61 ms. hs asked about the logical replication slot lag that bit us
in the 15.4 upgrade; dm confirmed the slot monitor now alerts at 200 MB
instead of 2 GB, which would have caught it.

ay pushed for a date. The 12 March release window is already a low-traffic
Thursday and marketing has nothing scheduled.

DECISION: ship the Postgres 16 upgrade to production in the 12 March
release window; dm owns the runbook.
ACTION: dm to publish the upgrade runbook, due Mon 9 Mar.
ACTION: hs to schedule the read-replica cutover rehearsal, due Wed 11 Mar.
QUESTION: do we need a separate rollback window for the analytics replica?
Left open -- dm to bring the restore timings next week.

--- 2. Canary deploy rollout ---

ln reported that six of the nine payments services now deploy through the
canary pipeline. The three holdouts are ledger-writer, fx-quote and the old
settlement batch job. ln's view: ledger-writer and fx-quote are a week of
work each, the batch job is a rewrite.

ay wanted a deadline rather than a plan. tk noted that the incident review
board has been asking for exactly this since January.

DECISION: canary deploys become mandatory for all services in the payments
group starting 1 April; hs owns the policy text.
ACTION: ln to add canary gates to the payments CI templates, due Fri 13 Mar.
QUESTION: who pages when a canary stalls at 5 percent? Unresolved -- the
current runbook says "the deploying team" and nobody believes it.

--- 3. Redis eviction incident follow-up ---

tk read out the timeline from the 27 February session-loss incident: the
session cluster hit its 8 GB maxmemory ceiling at 14:12, allkeys-lru began
evicting, and roughly 40 000 users were signed out over eleven minutes.
Headroom at the time of the incident was under 4 percent.

pri asked whether more memory just delays the same failure. tk agreed it
does, but the growth curve gives us two quarters.

DECISION: raise the Redis maxmemory ceiling from 8 GB to 12 GB on the
session cluster; tk owns the change.
ACTION: tk to file the capacity change request, due Thu 5 Mar.
QUESTION: should session data move off Redis entirely in H2? Parked for
the architecture review.

final-round interviews and one has not been posted.

DECISION: defer the second SRE requisition to Q3; ay owns the update to
the plan.
ACTION: ay to update the hiring plan document, due Tue 17 Mar.

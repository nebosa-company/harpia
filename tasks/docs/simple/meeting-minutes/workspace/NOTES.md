# Working notes

Raw scribe notes land in `notes/` straight from the meeting; nobody tidies
them afterwards, so the markers (`DECISION:`, `ACTION:`, `QUESTION:`) are
the only reliable structure. Handles in the notes map to people through
`roster.csv` — the same handle set has been stable for two years.

Circulated minutes are read by people who were not in the room, so a name
they cannot resolve is worse than no minutes at all.

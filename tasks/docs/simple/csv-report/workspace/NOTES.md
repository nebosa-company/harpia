# Support reporting notes

The quarterly ticket export is the raw feed from the helpdesk. Tickets
closed as `duplicate` are the same customer problem counted twice; they
have always been dropped before any number goes in front of the support
leads, and the resolution-hours field on them is empty anyway.

Averages are quoted to one decimal place. Nobody believes more precision
than that and everybody argues about it.

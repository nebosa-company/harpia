# Contact list notes

Three systems, three schemas, three sets of habits. The CRM is the record
of truth for names and companies because a human types them; the events
platform is next best; the newsletter list is self-service and its company
field is whatever the subscriber felt like typing that day.

Phone numbers are the opposite story — the CRM often has none, and any
number is better than none, so we keep the best one we can find.

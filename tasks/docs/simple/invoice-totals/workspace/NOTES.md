# Billing reconciliation notes

Finance reconciles the invoice folder against the ledger every month and
the reconciliation is only useful if the arithmetic is exact — a cent of
drift anywhere and the whole run is re-done by hand.

Voided lines are the usual source of drift. They stay on the document,
they look like every other line, and they must not be billed.

Pricing

Surveys are quoted per site, but the three packages below cover most of what we are asked for. Mobilisation is charged separately and is stated on every quote before you accept it.

Single survey

EUR 4,200

  • One site, one visit
  • Processed data and a written report
  • Raw measurements delivered with the report

Monitoring

EUR 2,900 per visit

  • Repeat visits on a fixed interval
  • Change detection against the first survey
  • Uncertainty stated for every movement figure

Framework

Quoted per year

  • Agreed rates across a portfolio of sites
  • Priority scheduling within five working days
  • Annual calibration report for every instrument used

What is included

Field time, processing, the report and the raw measurements. Travel outside the Netherlands is quoted separately.

Terms

  • Prices exclude VAT.
  • Quotes are valid for ninety days.
  • Cancellation inside five working days is charged at half rate.

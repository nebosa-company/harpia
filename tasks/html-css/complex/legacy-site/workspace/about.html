About

Northbeam was founded in 2011 by two hydrographic surveyors who were tired of handing clients a PDF they could not check.

We are eleven people: six in the field, three in processing, and two who keep the instruments calibrated and the boats afloat.

Instruments

Multibeam echosounders, terrestrial laser scanners and a total station older than most of the staff.

How we work

  • Every figure is delivered with its uncertainty.
  • Raw measurements stay with the client, not with us.
  • Processing steps are recorded and reproducible.

Northbeam BV, Rotterdam. Surveys since 2011.

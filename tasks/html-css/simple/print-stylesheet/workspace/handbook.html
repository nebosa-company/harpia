Incident response

An incident starts the moment someone declares one. Nobody needs permission to declare, and no declaration is ever held against the person who made it.

Declaring

Post in the incident channel with the severity, the user-visible symptom and the time you first saw it. The bot opens a document and a call.

Roles

Three roles are filled before anything else: incident commander, comms, and scribe. See the role definitions for what each one owns.

Commander

Owns the decision log. Does not debug.

Comms

Owns the status page and the customer-facing timeline. Publishes on a fixed cadence even when there is nothing new.

After

The review happens within five working days, and the document is public inside the company. See the review template.
